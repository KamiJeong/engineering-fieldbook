# GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

## Term and aliases

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization, called 생성형 엔진 최적화 in Korean.

## Definition

Activities intended to improve how content appears and is used when generative AI combines sources into answers. The original research addresses content visibility in these responses and proposes evaluation methods.[^geo-paper]

## Understand through an example

**Assumptions:** A fictional pottery studio publishes eligibility, class size, total cost, and collection timing. This supplies concrete evidence for a comparison of weekend beginner classes. It is not an observed increase in citations or bookings.

## Avoid confusion

Here GEO does not mean geographic targeting or local search. It overlaps with AEO; Google treats generative search optimization as part of SEO. Do not assume the original research results reproduce across every platform.[^geo-paper][^google-ai]
