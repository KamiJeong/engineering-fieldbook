# SEO: Search Engine Optimization

## Term and aliases

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, called 검색 엔진 최적화 in Korean.

## Definition

Activities that help engines understand pages and help users discover them through search. This includes titles and information that help people decide whether to visit.[^seo]

## Understand through an example

**Assumptions:** Rename a fictional studio page from “A special experience” to “Beginner pottery workshop — Haru Studio.” This makes the service easier to identify; it is not a measured ranking improvement.

## Avoid confusion

SEO is not the same as buying search advertising. Optimization does not guarantee indexing or first place. Its fundamentals remain relevant to generative AI search.[^seo][^google-ai]
