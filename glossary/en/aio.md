# AIO: distinguishing AI Optimization from AI Overviews

## Term and aliases

This repository uses **AI Optimization** as its working meaning of AIO. Other material uses AIO for AI Overviews or AI Overview Optimization, so check the expanded name.[^aio-usage][^aio-alternate]

## Definition

Here it is a broad perspective on discovery and brand communication through AI, informed by FGS Global's usage. It is not a common standard across all platforms or a mandatory stage after SEO.[^aio-usage]

## Understand through an example

**Assumptions:** A studio's website and booking information list different class durations. The owner verifies the real duration, reconciles the sources, and checks AI descriptions. This hypothetical example improves consistency; it does not guarantee immediate changes in AI answers.

## Avoid confusion

Google AI Overviews is an AI summary feature in Search. Distinguish the product feature from AI Optimization activities. Define target services and expected results in an AIO request. Using AI to generate text is different from being selected as a source in its answers.[^ai-features]
