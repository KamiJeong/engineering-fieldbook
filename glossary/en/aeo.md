# AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

## Term and aliases

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization, called 답변 엔진 최적화 in Korean.

## Definition

Activities that make content easier for systems to find, understand, and use when answering users' questions. HubSpot uses the term broadly to include generative AI answers and citations.[^hubspot]

## Understand through an example

**Assumptions:** A studio page asks “Can I collect my piece the same day?” and answers “Firing is required; collection is approximately four weeks later.” This hypothetical example communicates a necessary condition; it is not observed search or AI exposure.

## Avoid confusion

AEO means more than adding questions and overlaps with GEO. A short answer or FAQ does not guarantee selection. Publishers cannot designate their pages as Google featured snippets either.[^hubspot][^snippets]
